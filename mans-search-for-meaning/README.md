# Man's Search for Meaning — Concept Map

An interactive, mind-map-style concept map for Viktor E. Frankl's *Man's Search for Meaning*, built as a single self-contained `knowledge-graph.html` page.

Ten themes radiate from the book's title — Decisions, Psychosomatic, Fear, Meaning / Purpose, Success, Humour, Cold Curiosity, Psychology, Tragic Optimism, and Love — each expandable into the book's own quotes, parables, and ideas (the stages of a prisoner's psychological reaction, the parable of Death in Teheran, logotherapy's three roads to meaning, the tragic triad, and more). Dotted arrows trace the associations Frankl draws between them — meaning, suffering, spirituality and happiness; love and the will to meaning; freedom and determinism.

Open `knowledge-graph.html` in a browser: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click any topic to expand or collapse it, or use the theme picker to jump straight to a branch.
