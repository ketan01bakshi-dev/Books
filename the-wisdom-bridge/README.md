# The Wisdom Bridge — Concept Map

An interactive, mind-map-style concept map for Daaji Kamlesh D. Patel's *The Wisdom Bridge: Nine Principles to a Life that Echoes in the Hearts of Your Loved Ones*, built as a single self-contained `knowledge-graph.html` page — the same engine and interaction model as [Man's Search for Meaning's concept map](../mans-search-for-meaning/knowledge-graph.html).

Eleven branches radiate from the book's title — Dear Parents, the nine Principles, and Closing — each opening into its chapters. Two chapters currently carry notes: Chapter 5 (*Becoming a Parent*, on will, faith, confidence and doubt) and Chapter 8 (*Ashtavakra, Abhimanyu and the Scientific Theory of Foetal Origins*, on the heart, the soul and the body's energy channels). Dotted arrows trace the associations the book draws within and across those ideas — doubt poisoning the will, preparation eliminating doubt, the heart as seat of the soul.

Open `knowledge-graph.html` in a browser: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click any topic to expand or collapse it, or use the theme picker to jump straight to a principle.

`app.js` / `book.js` / `index.html` / `styles.css` are a separate, earlier living map of the same nine principles; `learnings.md` is the running notebook of quotes and notes the concept map draws on; `knowledge-graph.json` is the original graph-database export the concept map's data was built from.
